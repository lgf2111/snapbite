Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version. SnapBite stores the meals you log, the profile you set, and your encrypted AI key so it can read your log back to you. It does not run ads, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sell or share your data. You can delete any meal, or everything, at any time.

What is stored

  • Your Telegram user id and chat id, so the bot knows whose log is whose.
  • Your meals: the title, the foods, their estimated nutrition, timestamps, and any note you add. Edits you make are stored too.
  • Your profile, if you set one: sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level and goal. These are used to compute your daily targets.
  • Your weight check-ins, if you use adaptive targets.
  • Your AI provider choice and API key. The key is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is written to the database and decrypted only in memory while handling your own request. It is never returned to any client and never logged.
  • Saved meals you star, and the settings you change.
  • Feedback you send with /feedback, and short error records used to fix faults.
  • For a photo you send: Telegram's file reference (file_id), so the same photo can be shown back to you in your log.

What is not stored

  • Meal photos themselves. The image is fetched from Telegram, sent to your AI provider for analysis, and discarded. Only Telegram's own file reference is kept, which means the photo is not hosted by SnapBite.
  • Nothing from other chats. The bot only reads messages sent to it directly.
  • No ad networks, no third-party analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, no contact-list upload. The web app is a static page; the app itself talks only to SnapBite's own backend.

Who else sees it

Three parties are involved in making SnapBite work, and nothing is shared beyond them:

  • Telegram delivers your messages and hosts the photos.
  • Your chosen AI provider (Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint) receives the meal photo or text and your prompt, under your own key, in order to analyse it. Their own privacy terms apply to that request. SnapBite does not send your history to them — only the meal being analysed.
  • Cloudflare runs the backend and stores the database, as infrastructure.

How long it is kept

Meals, profile, saved meals and settings stay until you delete them. Error records are trimmed as they age and are kept only to diagnose faults. Feedback is kept until it has been handled and then removed.

Deleting your data

  • One meal — swipe it away in the app, or delete it from the meal screen.
  • Everything — send /delete to the bot and confirm. This removes your meals, profile, saved meals, settings and your stored AI key. It cannot be undone.
  • A copy first — send /export to the bot to receive your log as a JSON file before you delete anything.

Children

SnapBite is not intended for anyone under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for children.

Estimates, not medical advice

Every nutrition number in SnapBite is an estimate produced by a model or a reference table. It is editable for exactly that reason. It is not medical or dietary advice, and it should not be used to manage a medical condition without a professional.

Changes

If what SnapBite stores changes materially, this page is updated and the date above changes. The code that implements all of this is public at github.com/lgf2111/snapbite.
