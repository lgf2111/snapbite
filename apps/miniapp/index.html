Snap a photo. Log your meal.

SnapBite is a lightweight, Telegram-native food logger. Send a photo — or just describe what you ate — and get calories, protein, carbs and fat back in seconds. Every value is an editable estimate, and it runs on your own AI key.

What it does

📸

Photo to nutrition

Send a photo to the bot; a vision model identifies the foods, portions, and rough calories + protein / carbs / fat.

✍️

No photo? Just describe it

"Two eggs, sourdough, half an avocado" runs through the same pipeline. Logging shouldn't need a camera.

🏷️

Labels & barcodes

If the photo shows a nutrition label or barcode, SnapBite reads it and uses the exact values instead of guessing.

✏️

Always editable

Everything is an estimate you can correct — reply "add a coke" or "the rice was double" and it re-runs the numbers.

🍽️

Shared a dish?

Say "divide by 4 pax" and only your share is logged — the meal title shows the split, so the count stays honest.

🎯

Targets that learn

Daily calorie + macro targets, then adaptive goals: log your weight and SnapBite tunes your target to your real trend.

🔥

Streaks & weekly recap

See your logging streak in every confirmation, and opt in to a short Sunday summary of your week.

🧑‍🍳

Ask the coach

"/coach am I low on protein today?" — answered in a sentence or two from your own logged data, on demand.

⭐

Re-log a favourite

Star a meal in the app, then send "/saved 1" to log it again. No photo, no AI call, no cost.

⏰

Meal reminders

Opt-in nudges for breakfast, lunch and dinner at times you choose, in your own timezone.

➕

Add a meal by hand

No photo, no AI, no key: type the name and macros in the app and it logs like any other meal.

🔑

Bring your own key

AI runs on your own provider key (Gemini, OpenAI, or DeepSeek) — a meal costs a fraction of a cent.

What it looks like in chat

You: two eggs, sourdough toast, half an avocado SnapBite: ~430 kcal · 22g protein · 38g carbs · 21g fat — tap to edit You: the toast was double SnapBite: updated — ~560 kcal · 26g protein · 52g carbs · 24g fat

Inside the app

How it works

1

Open the bot

Start @SnapBiteAI_bot in Telegram and add your AI key in Settings.

Getting a free key takes about a minute →

2

Send a meal — photo or words

The bot analyses it and logs the meal, editing its "Analyzing…" message into the result. Or add one by hand in the app, no key needed.

3

Review & correct

Open the Mini App to see your day against your targets, edit any meal, and track progress.

Your data

AI analysis runs on your own provider key — SnapBite never takes on model cost for you, and your key is stored encrypted.

Meal photos are kept only via Telegram's own file reference and shown back to you; image bytes aren't stored on our side.

Nutrition numbers are estimates and stay fully editable — you're always in control of your log.

No ads, no third-party analytics, no selling your data. Read the full privacy note →
